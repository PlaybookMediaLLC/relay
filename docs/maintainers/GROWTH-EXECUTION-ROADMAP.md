# Relay growth execution roadmap — founder-focused direction

**Strategy authority:** [vision.md](../../vision.md), updated November 7, 2026.  
**Audience:** independent founders of software businesses with source-controlled acquisition websites.  
**Execution evidence:** [existing RFC inventory and IRFC traceability](RFC-MILESTONE-TRACEABILITY.md).  
**Status:** product re-prioritization, not proof that planned features or paid adoption exist.

## Decision

Relay's first outcome is **an evidence-backed organic-acquisition opportunity that an independent founder approves, ships through their own website, verifies live, and subsequently observes**. Optimize for completing that loop, not for owning release operations, managing every product fact, duplicating Shots, or adding more SEO dashboards.

Release regression protection still matters. The existing Protect engineering slices remain valid and can be completed when they support acquisition reliability or a separately validated Protect offer. They are **not** a universal prerequisite for an already supported SEO opportunity → GitHub change → measurement workflow.

Do not abandon the inherited OpenSEO services, change approvals, site audits, GSC/GA4 integrations, DataForSEO budgets, experiment history or dual-database safety model. Do not recreate finished historical RFC work to satisfy new milestone labels.

## A practical first growth execution slice (M13)

### Founder setup

- Establish product URL, target customer, high-value pages and one conversion event, using the existing project/settings structure.
- Connect GitHub and GSC where authorized; optional GA4 and DataForSEO research are bounded and disclosed.
- If Search Console has no usable history, report the limitation and offer a small site-audit/public-demand path, **not** an invented traffic forecast.

### Select work

- Start with a clearly relevant existing-page opportunity: weak title/snippet, missing important answer, underperforming query, confirmed indexability problem, or a supported source-backed copy issue.
- Show the baseline, evidence date, confidence, why it matters for the founder's audience, cost to investigate, expected technical postcondition and risk.
- Reuse opportunity detectors and existing review. Do not require all Protect monitoring snapshots or cross-system release facts before an ordinary SEO proposal.

### Execute and verify

- Require supported Next.js/static/known-source adapter and exact source mapping.
- Show the immutable diff and separate approval to open PR. Customer retains merge/deploy authority.
- Pin exact revision, observe deployment where reliable, independently inspect the live destination and record blocked/inconclusive states.
- Open an existing Search Console experiment or equivalent observation only with sufficient data and the established 28-day baseline, 7-day exclusion and 28-day observation window where applicable. Keep search-indexing lag and causal uncertainty visible.

### Commercial acceptance

- Demonstrate the flow on a Playbook site first for engineering confidence; external paid independent-founder evidence is still required.
- Track founder setup time, provider calls and costs, time to reviewed proposal, time to independently verified production state, false proposals and actual renewal behavior.
- **The initial promise is reliable completion of approved website work, not guaranteed growth in clicks or revenue.**

## Existing IRFCs: preserve source truth, change investment priority

**Keep and reuse early:**
- Historical RFC-0002–0006 and RFC-0010/0015 for opportunities, investigation, publishing, site checks and measurement.
- Protect IRFC-0001–0006 for improved monitoring, baseline, approved fixes and original-rule verification as warranted by a paid Protect pilot or a real growth safety gap. IRFC-0001 implementation is merged; live acceptance remains distinct.
- IRFC-0023's history/cooldown scope and IRFC-0026's carefully bounded existing-page growth campaigns. Bring *parts* forward if they unblock M13 or repeat paid work; do not reimplement the whole old program.

**Use narrowly when required:**
- IRFC-0024/0025 conversion/journey verification when it addresses acquisition drop-off or missing conversion events; avoid safe-sounding browser actions that actually create leads, sales or payments.
- IRFC-0018's reviewer experience only to show a useful, evidence-backed work cycle, not to create a second broad operations cockpit.
- IRFC-0010/0011 permission/connector constraints when expanding an existing integration. Build a minimal adapter, not a generalized connector platform.
- IRFC-0013's scoped factual evidence as a guardrail only for claims Relay proposes to add. Don't build a second product-fact registry alongside an inherited one.

**On hold, not deleted:**
- IRFC-0009–0019 as an unconditional Product Reality Engine → standalone Sync program. Recover specific authority/fact/verification slices only after a founder workflow justifies them.
- IRFC-0020/0021 Relay-owned launch lifecycle and release packets. Existing-page SEO checks may be reused; **Shots** owns actual release kits and communication.
- IRFC-0022 broad read-only change-impact APIs and new external subscriptions. Reuse existing MCP research tools first.
- IRFC-0027 bulk or new-page product ambition until existing-page execution proves paid demand; when used, one human-reviewed fact-backed page at a time.
- IRFC-0028/0029 support and sales enablement unless a founder-acquisition use case proves a distinct need.
- IRFC-0030 competitor response until relevant customer use and cost; IRFC-0031/0032 optional hosted builds/delegation require explicit safety-and-demand approval.

## Portfolio boundary and future integrations

Relay may read an approved product description or documentation source to avoid inventing features on an SEO page. That does not make it the owner of product launch work. The first cross-product handoff, if useful, should be a small approved request or link to **Shots** for screenshots, launch announcements and social-ready assets. Verify/Warmbly owns contact quality and outbound. A future Playbook Operator may coordinate their tasks; do not build a competing general agent supervisor in Relay.

## Governance and next decision

Before making another broad RFC mandatory, answer: **Which independent founder pays, which acquisition job improves, what exact proof closes the work, how much does it cost, and why existing OpenSEO/Codex/GitHub tools aren't enough?**

Review the M13 pilot and M7 payment/renewal evidence before escalating M14, generalized PRE, managed CMS writing or portfolio-wide campaign orchestration. It's acceptable to stop or discard an unproven module while keeping its useful internal code.

The actual milestone authority is the live GitHub Milestone record; this document is its human-readable strategy and compatibility map. Update both when decisions change.
